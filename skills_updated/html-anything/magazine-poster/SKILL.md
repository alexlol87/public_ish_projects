【Template: Magazine Poster】
【Intent】A tall poster in newsprint editorial style. It should read like a full broadsheet page.
【Layout】
- Dateline bar at the top (publication / date / issue)
- Oversized serif headline (with a struck-through word and an italic accent)
- Two-column body text
- 6 numbered sections, each with a small heading, 1-2 paragraphs and a pull-quote
- Byline and a small ornament at the bottom
【Design details】
- Paper feel: warm grey cream background with a fine dot pattern, black text
- Fonts: Playfair Display + IBM Plex Serif + JetBrains Mono
